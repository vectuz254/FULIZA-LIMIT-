Kenya's #1 Overdraft Service. Borrow. Repay. Thrive.

Fuliza is Safaricom's overdraft facility that allows M-PESA users to complete transactions even when they have insufficient funds. Understand exactly how overdraft charges work — and how much agents earn.

View Fuliza Brackets

What is Fuliza?

📱

How It Works

When your M-PESA balance is insufficient, Fuliza automatically tops up your transaction. You repay from your next M-PESA deposit.

📅

Daily Charges

Fuliza charges a daily maintenance fee based on the outstanding overdraft amount until it is fully repaid.

✅

Auto Repayment

Any money received into your M-PESA is automatically used to repay your Fuliza balance first before you can access it.

🔓

How to Activate

Dial *234# or open M-PESA app → Fuliza → Activate. Your limit is set based on your M-PESA usage history.
